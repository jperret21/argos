One observation, one record

The useful information stays close to the frame.

  1. 01Connect the instrument

    Telescope profile, position and camera are chosen once, visibly.

  2. 02Identify the field

    WCS, catalogue sources, magnitudes and selected stars belong on the image.

  3. 03Observe the source

    Acquire a sequence and inspect the evolving measurement without claiming final calibration.

  4. 04Review the session

    Open the frames behind a point, inspect the curve and hand the complete record to post-processing.
