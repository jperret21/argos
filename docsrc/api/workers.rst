========================
``argos.workers``
========================

.. admonition:: Legacy: the retired Qt app

   not part of the 0.5.0 desktop app; it stays as the parity reference of the
   server's tests (:doc:`../ARCHITECTURE`, "The retired Qt app, and why it
   stays").
