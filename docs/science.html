Method / ARGOS 0.4.1

Method & limits.

The measurement ARGOS runs on every frame, the files it writes, and what 0.4.1 has not validated.

Read this first

The live curve is a quick-look: raw subs, no dark, flat or bias, no registration, JD_UTC midpoints. It exists to catch clouds, saturation, drift and a bad comparison star while the night can still be saved.

The publishable measurement is produced afterwards from the raw FITS, in Siril and star_var_script. Keep the session folder.

01

Per-frame pipeline

  1. Write the sub. Linear 16-bit CFA FITS, GRBG, straight from the driver — never the displayed image. Headers carry DATE-OBS, DATE-AVG (exposure midpoint), MJD-OBS, MJD-AVG, EXPTIME, EGAIN, OFFSET, CCD-TEMP, AIRMASS, MOONSEP, OBJCTRA/DEC, SITELAT/LONG, FOCALLEN, APTDIA, FOCRATIO, BAYERPAT and IMAGETYP.
  2. Solve. ASTAP, external, on the extracted green plane. Automatic solving re-runs at most every 20 s, or after a slew. A failed solve keeps the last good WCS rather than blanking the overlays.
  3. Place the apertures. Target, comparison and check positions project through the WCS, then re-centroid. A rigid rotation-and-translation fit against the reference geometry absorbs alt-az field rotation and mount drift, re-fit every frame so centroid noise does not accumulate.
  4. Measure. Circular aperture of 2.5 × FWHM; median sky annulus from 8 to 12 green pixels. Uncertainty from the CCD equation with 1.5 e⁻/px read noise and the driver's EGAIN. Saturation flags at the profile's linearity limit — 50 000 ADU, not full well.
  5. Reduce differentially. The zero point is the mean of (catalogue − instrumental) over the VSP comparisons in band TG. Comparisons further than max(2.5 × 1.4826 × MAD, 10 mmag) from the median are dropped, at most twice; with fewer than two survivors the point is flagged uncalibrated. An unnormalised target/ensemble flux ratio is recorded alongside the magnitude, so a transit needs no catalogue magnitudes at all.
Why the green planeMeasurements use the raw GRBG green samples, averaged (G1+G2)/2 per 2×2 tile. They are recorded photosites; the interpolated RGB view is for orientation only. ASTAP solves this plane, and every overlay coordinate is expressed in it.

02

Diagnostics worth reading

  • Check star. A star assumed constant, measured against the same ensemble. It moves when the ensemble or the sky moves, not when the target does.
  • Comparison report. Every comparison gets a leave-one-out differential curve. It is reported stable only with at least 10 unsaturated points, robust scatter ≤ 0.10 mag and formal error ≤ 0.10 mag. Role changes are appended to photometry_selection_history.jsonl.
  • A shift common to target, check and comparisons is transparency, tracking or focus. No ensemble removes it.

bjd_tdb is computed with astropy from the exposure midpoint and the configured site, so a transit timing does not have to be reconstructed downstream. The AAVSO export writes the science target only — comparison curves are diagnostics and must never be submitted as observations.

04

Known limits in 0.4.1

  • One validated profile. The S30 Pro. The S30 and S50 ship with published optics but an assumed GRBG pattern, an inherited EGAIN curve and S30 Pro saturation thresholds — see Instruments.
  • ASTAP is external. ARGOS bundles neither the executable nor a star database. An unsuccessful or implausible solution invalidates every identity overlay.
  • Catalogue coverage is bounded. A per-field budget of 100 to 1 600 objects caps the Gaia and SIMBAD identities considered. A missing marker is a budget, not a non-detection.
  • No unattended operation. No weather safety, no recovery after a device or network interruption. Stay with the telescope.
  • No published precision figure. The missing evidence is a multi-night comparison against star_var_script on the same raw frames — not another feature.
