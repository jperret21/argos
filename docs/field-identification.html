Field identification / ARGOS 0.4.1

Identify a solved field.

Solve a frame, read the catalogue overlays, and assign the target, comparison and check roles.

Two operations, not one

Plate solving maps image pixels to sky coordinates. Field identification asks which catalogue objects sit at those coordinates. The second is worthless when the first is wrong.

Overlays never modify the FITS.

01 · SolveField → Identify field

Centre, scale and grid must match the stars.

02 · FilterGaia G ≤ 18.0

Ten layer chips, one slider, one budget.

03 · AssignTarget · Comparison · Check

At least two comparisons.

01

Start from a trustworthy solution

  1. Open a full-resolution light frame in Observe, or use File → Open FITS image… to inspect an existing frame.
  2. Choose Field → Identify field. ARGOS calls the ASTAP set in Settings → Astrometry and solves on the extracted green plane.
  3. Check the solved centre, image scale and coordinate grid against recognisable stars. If markers consistently miss the centroids, fix the solve — changing the stretch, zoom or debayer mode does not fix a coordinate error.
Solved ≠ identifiedA detection proves a source exists, not what it is. The Target, Comparison and Check buttons stay disabled until the field is solved and the clicked star has reliable coordinates.

04

Assign scientific roles

Click a marker: catalogue identity, RA/Dec, magnitudes, and image measurements such as FWHM, HFD, SNR and saturation where available. Drag the card by its title bar, resize it by its bottom-right corner.

ButtonMeaning
TargetThe star whose differential magnitude is measured.
Comparison starA reference star contributing to the ensemble zero point.
Check starA star expected to be constant, measured against the ensemble to expose its instability. It does not check focus.
RemoveTakes this star out of the saved set.
DismissCloses the card; roles already assigned are kept.

Once a field catalogue is available, ARGOS can propose comparisons from VSP candidates and image-quality checks. Inspect separation, brightness, saturation and blending before accepting. At least two comparisons are required; a measurement with fewer is flagged only N comparison(s).

05

Catalogue, cache and privacy

The bundled Messier/NGC/IC catalogue is local and read-only. Gaia, SIMBAD/CDS, AAVSO and NASA are read from cache first; with a provider enabled, ARGOS requests only the sky cone the field needs. Paths are in Settings → Catalogues · data.

Refresh deletes the selected local cache and downloads nothing; the next explicit lookup refetches it if there is internet. With providers disabled, field identification is cache-only.

06

When the answer is uncertain

  • No marker on a bright star: fainter than the Gaia G limit slider, deeper than the cached Gaia depth, over the identification budget, or in a disabled catalogue.
  • A marker in empty sky: do not assign a role. Recheck the ASTAP centre, scale and rotation on a full-resolution frame.
  • The card says manual measurement: a local source with no sufficiently close catalogue match. It has no identity.
  • A provider does not answer: work from cache or retry later. An empty layer is not a negative survey.
  • One object, several names: the card's full identity is authoritative; overlay labels are abbreviated.
