Instruments / ARGOS 0.4.1

Instrument support.

Three Seestar profiles, two packages, and the numbers each profile assumes.

Pick the right profile

A telescope profile is a set of optical and sensor constants. Selecting the wrong one does not fail — it silently changes the plate scale under every magnitude you measure.

ValidatedSeestar S30 Pro

30 mm f/5.3, IMX585, 3.74″/px. The model ARGOS was built against.

UnvalidatedSeestar S30 · S50

Published optics, assumed Bayer pattern, inherited gain curve.

Not in 0.4.1S50 Pro · DwarfLab

No profile ships for either. Reasons below.

Seestar S5050 mm, 250 mm, f/5.0IMX462, 1920×1080, 2.9 µm2.39″/px1.3° × 0.7°Unvalidated

All three assume a GRBG Bayer pattern, a 12-bit ADC, 60 000 ADU full well and a 50 000 ADU linearity limit — the threshold the saturation flag actually uses — with internal filter slots Dark, IR and LP, and access-point address 10.0.0.1 on port 32323. Photometry is measured on the green plane, so the scale that matters for an aperture is twice the figure above: 7.48″, 7.98″ and 4.79″ per green pixel.

What “unvalidated” means here

Three things on the S30 and S50 are assumed rather than measured: the Bayer pattern is taken to be GRBG, the Seestar gain scale and EGAIN curve have had no photon-transfer calibration, and the ADC depth and saturation thresholds are inherited from the S30 Pro. Connect, frame, plan and acquire freely. Do not publish a precision figure from either until those are measured on hardware.

“Validated” on the S30 Pro means every value is confirmed against the code it replaced — not that a night of photometry has been checked on sky.

Not in 0.4.1

The Seestar S50 Pro has a publishable telephoto specification — 50 mm, 260 mm, 2.9 µm, 3840×2160, about 2.30″/px — but its raw CFA layout and its gain, linearity and saturation behaviour are unverified, so no profile ships rather than one that looks plausible. DwarfLab support is a roadmap intention with no implementation in this release.

02

Profile checking at connect

ARGOS compares the driver's reported camera name and resolution against the selected profile when you connect, and warns on a mismatch. It reports; it never switches, because changing the plate scale under a running series is worse than the mismatch itself.

Resolution is not enoughThe S30 and S50 share a 1920×1080 sensor, so ARGOS cannot tell them apart from the driver alone. Confirm the model yourself.

05

First-night hardware checklist

  1. Select the exact model in Settings → Equipment · camera — S30 Pro, S30 or S50.
  2. Connect. Telescope, Camera, Filter Wheel and Focuser must all read Ready, and the wheel should report its three slots: Dark, IR, LP.
  3. Acquire one full-resolution light frame and read its header. FOCRATIO must be 5.3 on an S30 Pro, APTDIA 30, FOCALLEN 160, BAYERPAT GRBG. A wrong FOCRATIO means the wrong profile is active.
  4. Solve that frame with ASTAP before trusting any overlay or photometry role.
  5. Run a short test sequence before the real one.
